Good Night George

George haunts the pirates of the caribbean ride. Employees have to say good-morning George and goodnight George when opening and closing the ride, or else the ride will malfunction and animatronics will stop working.

Witchcraft Book

On the haunted mansion ride the mystical book was originally a real seance book from the fourteenth century book of witchcraft. Employees were noticing the book on the floor and turned upside down, they could not just keep it still, so they finally replaced it.

Walter's Office

The legend goes that when Walt was at the park he would always leave the lamp on to indicate to Cast Members that he was around. Cast members have spotted the light turning on by itself, and now the lamp is left on in memory of Walt.

Abandoned Waterpark
